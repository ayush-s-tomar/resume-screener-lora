Resume screening: your judgment

You will see 36 short, fictional resumes, each written for a target role. For each one, choose how well the candidate fits that role:

  • Strong: you would shortlist this person straight away.
  • Moderate: worth a closer look or a screening call.
  • Weak: you would not move forward for this role.

There are no trick questions, so go with your judgment. It takes about 15 minutes. Please do not discuss your answers with anyone until everyone has finished. Your answers stay in this browser until you download them at the end.
