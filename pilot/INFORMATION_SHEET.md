# Resume checker pilot: a 10-minute survey

I am a B.Tech student building a small research project, a resume screening model called resume-screener-lora. I would like to know whether it is useful to students who are about to apply for jobs, and where it is wrong.

What you do (about 10 minutes): open the demo at https://resume-screener-finetune.onrender.com, choose one of the sample resumes or paste your own resume with the mask option ticked (names and addresses are not masked, so remove them if you prefer), decide what you expect BEFORE you press the button, run it, then answer this form.

Your data: this form asks for no name, email or phone number and keeps nothing on a server. Your answers stay in your browser until you download a small file and send it to me. I will publish only combined results. Please do not write personal details in the comment box. The file has no name on it, so I cannot remove your answers once you have sent it; if you change your mind, simply do not send the file.

The demo sends the text you submit to a third-party service (Groq) to be scored; its Privacy panel explains what is kept. If you are not comfortable using your own resume, use a sample one: that is just as useful to me.

This is a research demo, not a hiring tool. Its scores can be wrong, and they say nothing about your worth or your chances. Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any time.

**Consent.** I am 18 or older, I have read the above, I understand that this is a student research project and that taking part is voluntary, and I agree that my answers (which contain no name or contact details) are combined with others and published as aggregate results.
