Hi! I'm testing a small resume checker I built (a research project, not a product) and I'd really value 10 minutes of your honest feedback, especially if you're about to apply for internships or jobs.

What to do:
1. Open https://resume-screener-finetune.onrender.com (it can take up to a minute to wake up).
2. Pick one of the sample resumes, or paste your own with the "mask" option ticked.
3. Before you press the button, decide how well you think the resume fits the role. Then run it and look at the score and the skills lists.
4. Open the attached file survey.html in your browser (Chrome or Edge), answer the questions, click "Download my answers", and send me the small file.

It is completely voluntary and anonymous: there is no name or email in the form, and I will publish only combined results. The demo sends the text to a third-party service (Groq), so use a sample resume if you'd rather not use your own. The scores can be wrong, and they say nothing about your worth or your chances. Thank you!
