# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarised here in plain words.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and welcoming to people of every background and experience level.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information, including real resumes.
- Spam, or anything that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, Ayush Singh Tomar, may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, warn people, or block accounts that do not follow these rules. To report a problem, use the contact options on https://github.com/ayush-s-tomar.
